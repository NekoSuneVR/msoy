Welcome to Whirled!

Whirled is a social online world for chat and multi-player games, powered by player-created content. Players can create characters, rooms and games and share them with the rest of this installation.

To get started, head into Whirled. Log in with your account details and use the in-world navigation to explore games, people, groups and your account settings.

Thanks for running and exploring this restored Whirled server.
